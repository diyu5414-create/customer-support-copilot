Question : My Galaxy Buds3 Pro left earbud won't connect. Is this a known issue, and has the same problem been resolved for another customer? What fixed it?
Routing  : {'intents': ['product', 'past_issue'], 'indexes': ['product_index', 'ticket_index'], 'reason': 'The question asks whether a product issue is known and whether the same problem was resolved for another customer.'}
Answer   : Yes. A known Galaxy Buds3 Pro issue is that the left earbud may not connect after pairing with a new phone. The 1 July 2026 guidance is to put both earbuds in the case, close the lid for 10 seconds, then reset them in the Galaxy Wearable app or hold both touchpads for 7 seconds. Pair them again as a new device.

A resolved customer case had the same problem after switching phones. The earbuds were still paired with the old phone; resetting them in the case and pairing again as a new device fixed it.

If the problem continues after the reset, the guidance says to treat it as defective under RR-003 within 48 hours of delivery, or as a warranty claim after that.

Question : A customer wants to change the address after the order shipped. What is the official process, and do we have a resolved case like this?
Routing  : {'intents': ['order', 'past_issue'], 'indexes': ['order_index', 'ticket_index'], 'reason': 'The question asks about the process for changing an address after shipping and whether there is a resolved case.'}
Answer   : **Official process (effective 1 July 2026):** Once an order has shipped, Dandes Electronics and the support team cannot change the delivery address. The customer can ask the courier to reschedule delivery to the same address, or refuse the delivery and place a new order with the correct address. A refund for a refused delivery follows policy SH-005.

**Resolved case:** Yes. In a resolved case involving a shipped headphone order, the customer refused delivery, placed a new order with the new address, and received a refund for the first order after the package reached the warehouse.

Question : What does policy RR-004 say?
Routing  : {'intents': ['policy'], 'indexes': ['policy_index'], 'reason': 'The question asks about a specific policy code, RR-004.'}
Answer   : RR-004 is the refund policy, effective 1 July 2026. Refunds begin after the returned product passes the quality check:

- UPI and wallet: 1–3 business days.
- Credit card, debit card, and net banking: 5–7 business days.
- Cash on Delivery: 5–7 business days after you add bank details on the Orders page.
- EMI: refunded to the card; the bank closes the EMI plan.

Question : A customer received a damaged phone and wants a refund instead of a replacement. What is the official process, and do we have a previous resolved case where a refund was issued?
Routing  : {'intents': ['policy', 'order', 'past_issue'], 'indexes': ['policy_index', 'order_index', 'ticket_index'], 'reason': 'The question asks for the official refund process for a damaged phone and a previous resolved refund case.'}
Answer   : Under the damaged-product policy effective 1 July 2026, report the damage within 48 hours of delivery using **Report a Problem** on the Orders page. Provide photos of the phone and box, plus an unboxing video if available. After pickup and a quality check, phones are generally handled with a replacement; a refund is available only if a replacement unit is unavailable.

There is a previous resolved case: in TCK-1002, an iPhone 16 arrived with a cracked screen. The customer reported it within 48 hours and provided photos and an unboxing video. After pickup and quality check, the same-colour replacement was unavailable, so a full refund was issued to the customer’s credit card. The case does not specify how long the refund took.
