Question : A customer wants to change the address after the order shipped. What is the official process, and do we have a resolved case like this?
Routing  : {'intents': ['order', 'past_issue'], 'indexes': ['order_index', 'ticket_index'], 'reason': 'The question asks about the process for changing an address after shipping and a resolved case like this.'}
Answer   : Once an order has shipped, Dandes and the support team can't change its delivery address. The customer can ask the courier to reschedule delivery to the same address, or refuse the delivery and place a new order with the correct address. A refused delivery is refunded under policy SH-005.

There is a resolved case: a customer with a shipped Sony WH-1000XM5 order refused delivery and placed a new order at their new address. The first order was refunded after the package reached the warehouse.

Question : What does policy RR-004 say?
Routing  : {'intents': ['policy'], 'indexes': ['policy_index'], 'reason': 'The question asks about a specific policy code, RR-004.'}
Answer   : Policy RR-004, dated 1 July 2026, says refunds begin after the returned product passes the quality check. UPI and wallet refunds take 1-3 business days; credit and debit card and net banking refunds take 5-7 business days. Cash on Delivery refunds are sent to the customer's bank account within 5-7 business days after bank details are added on the Orders page. EMI refunds go to the card, and the bank closes the EMI plan.

Answer   : For a damaged phone, report the issue within 48 hours of delivery using Report a Problem on the Orders page. Provide photos of the phone, product box, and shipping label, plus an unboxing video if available. The product will be picked up for a quality check. A replacement is provided first; a refund is issued if the same model and colour are unavailable. UPI and wallet refunds take 1-3 business days, while card and net banking refunds take 5-7 business days.

Yes. In resolved case TCK-1002, an iPhone 16 arrived with a cracked screen. The customer reported it within 48 hours and provided photos and an unboxing video. After pickup and quality check, the same-colour replacement was unavailable, so a full refund was issued to the customer's credit card.
